    Phased roadmap

    Twelve months, four phases, pilot-first
    Phase 1 · 0–3 mo

    Field Capture App, Digital ID & Registry, basic chain-of-custody ledger. One pilot site.

    Phase 2 · 3–6 mo

    Depot & warehouse module, assay capture, digital payments engine on mobile money.

    Phase 3 · 6–9 mo

    Transport tracker, export documentation hub, government & ICGLR interfaces.

    Phase 4 · 9–12 mo

    Buyer portal, compliance reporting suite, impact dashboard, trade finance.
