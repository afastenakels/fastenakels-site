Why this works

Diesel is the enemy

The mine burns diesel at $1,090–1,440 per tonne of copper produced — roughly 10x its grid power cost. Remote-mine diesel power runs $0.15–0.50/kWh. Solar + storage displaces it at $0.184, and the mine's own DCF shows the pain.

Paid in copper, not promises

The offtake gives us LME-grade cathode at a discount sized to cover the power bill. At just 300 t/month, 96% of the invoice is pre-collateralised in metal. We elect the quotation period (M+1 / M+3) and earn a trading margin on every tonne.

A proven counterparty

Site visit completed. Operating SXEW plant, active production, and a general manager who already runs a 50,000 t/yr sister operation. Expansion to 100,000 t/yr by 2030 is underway on a $758m programme.

Three ways to finance it

1 · Traditional project finance

Base case. 70% debt at 10% over 15 years, ~$47m equity cheque.

  • Equity IRR 17.1%, DSCR 1.65x
  • Consolidated 18.9–21.7% with trading
  • Keeps project and offtake

2 · Build-and-flip

A trading house funds construction, sells at COD to a solar asset manager, keeps the offtake.

  • At a 12% buyer return the flip loses ~$10m
  • Breakeven needs a buyer at ~11%
  • Real prize is retaining the offtake

3 · 100% self-funded

Full $162m as shareholder debt at 10%. Fastest, no lender consents.

  • All-in IRR ~11.3% incl. margin
  • Thin 1.3% spread over cost of funds
  • Fallback for speed only
Recommendation: proceed on Structure 1; market-test the COD exit in parallel; hold Structure 3 in reserve.

Layered protection if the mine defaults

LayerMechanism
1. Metal firstPower bill settled in copper we take title to at delivery — up to 100% pre-collateralised monthly
2. Prepayment4–6 months of power fees (~$14m) prepaid by the mine before construction notice-to-proceed
3. Termination indemnityOn default: outstanding debt + unamortised CAPEX + equity + breakage + demobilisation + 10% return
4. Security packageProjectCo share pledge; mortgage over the mining licence and its economic rights (OHADA/DRC law)
5. Receivables pledgePledge over amounts owed by the mine's senior offtaker — reaches cashflow at the source
6. CovenantsOperations & licence maintenance, no conflicting debt/offtake, monthly reporting, MAC notification
