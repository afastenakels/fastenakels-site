Chapter One

A site the market will not offer twice

Kacyiru is where Rwanda governs: the President's Office, the ministries, the UN agencies, embassy row. Through it runs the RN3, connecting the CBD to the Convention Centre district in minutes. On that road, beside the Embassy of Egypt, 7,853 sqm of titled, C3-zoned commercial land has come to market at $3–4 million, roughly $380–510 per square metre.

I have walked the site, pulled the zoning report, built the development case and stress-tested the numbers. The district visibly lacks extended-stay residences and Grade A offices; the demand is permanent, USD-linked and sits within walking distance. The full investor pack exists: model, memorandum, deck. What the project needs next is not analysis. It is a sponsor team strong enough to close it.

Chapter Three

The sponsor economics we would share

The structure is a single-asset Rwandan SPV. Investors put in $14.0m of equity alongside roughly $17.2m of senior debt, and receive a 10% preferred return. Above that hurdle, profits split 80% to investors and 20% to the sponsor side. The sponsor team also earns a market-standard 3% development management fee through the build. In the base case, that sponsor pool looks like this:

How that pool splits between us is a function of what each side puts at risk: seed capital, guarantees, balance sheet, and work. I have a view, and it is negotiable. In the bear case (rents 7.5% under model, exit at a 9.25% valuation rate) the project still returns a ~15% development profit on cost, the fee is still earned, and the promote thins. We would be paid the way sponsors should be: mostly for succeeding.

Chapter Four

What we would build

One address, four income engines, each matched to demand that already walks past the site: 90 upscale serviced residences for diplomatic and development-sector stays that run weeks to months; ~7,900 sqm of Grade A offices for the firms and donor programs that want to sit beside government; ~2,200 sqm of retail and dining animating the road; and ~1,700 sqm of conference and event space five minutes from the Kigali Convention Centre, with 230 parking bays below.

Mixed income staggers lease maturities and de-risks debt service. Offices and retail can phase first if the raise favors it. The building answers what Kacyiru measurably lacks, without competing head-on with the international hotel brands' new full-service supply.
