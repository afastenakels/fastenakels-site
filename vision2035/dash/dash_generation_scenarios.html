What each source actually cost CEET in 2025 (FCFA/kWh)

What new supply could cost (LCOE benchmarks, $/MWh)

Levelized cost per MWh, 2025–26 global benchmarks at 10% WACC (editable in the Excel). Orders of magnitude to be validated against offers: the Blitta PPA (54 FCFA ≈ $90/MWh incl. transport) sits above the solar benchmark.

Cost of firm capacity at the evening peak ($/kW-yr)

Annualized capex + fixed O&M of the asset that provides the 19h–22h MW. For a solar + battery plant that is the battery only (the solar capex is paid by the energy it sells). This is why solar alone cannot close the gap, and why 4h batteries, alone or paired with solar, are the cheapest firm MW. HFO and imports need no new capex but carry the highest energy cost and delivery risk.
